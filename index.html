Flight match tracker → Enter prices as teams sell. Probabilities use A/B/C tiers; EV and break-even update instantly.

Payouts, tiers & derby

Break-even = highest price at which buying a team outright has zero expected profit, counting your own bid into the pot. Ceiling applies the safety margin. Payouts: 20% of each flight pot goes to the derby, the rest pays 50/30/20; the derby pays 50/25/15/10 among flight winners. Unsold teams count at the flight's estimated price. Half buyback at half price carries exactly half the EV.
