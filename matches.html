Clinch = your points already exceed every other team's best possible finish (they win all remaining matches 5-up, including one against you). "Need" is the net points you must still bank for that to hold, assuming the worst in your remaining matches. Avg = points gained per match played. Proj = current points + Avg × matches remaining; the projected winner is the team with the highest Proj. Teams with no results yet show "–".
